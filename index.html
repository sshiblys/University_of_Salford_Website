About SEE

The University of Salford's largest school

Formed from three pioneering schools, SEE unites world-class research and teaching across science, engineering, computing, and the built and natural environment. Our £65m SEE Building — winner of the RIBA Award 2025 — provides 15,300 m² of state-of-the-art teaching and collaboration space.

  • 🔬
    World-leading researchFive specialist research centres tackling net zero, climate change, healthy living and smart cities.
  • 🤝
    Industry at the coreStudents and academics working on real-world projects with leading industry partners from day one.
  • 🌍
    Civic impactTransforming livelihoods and driving innovation across Greater Manchester and beyond.
  • 🏆
    Accredited excellenceBCS, RAeS, ICE and RICS-accredited degrees with outstanding graduate employment outcomes.
